Managing Rain Water --- Make Sri Lanka Prosperous

DI/Hydro/103(V) 2026/10/26

Significant heavy rainfall has been recorded across the catchment areas of the Gin Ganga Basin. Based on the prevailing rainfall conditions and the analysis of water levels recorded at the river gauging stations maintained by the Department of Irrigation within the Gin Ganga Basin, there is a risk of minor flooding occurring within the next 24 hours from now in the low-lying areas of the Gin Ganga Valley falling within the Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Thawalama, Nagoda, Baddegama and Welivitiya-Divithura. The public residing in these areas and motorists travelling through these areas are requested to exercise extreme caution in view of this situation. Disaster Management authorities are requested to take the necessary precautionary measures in this regard.
