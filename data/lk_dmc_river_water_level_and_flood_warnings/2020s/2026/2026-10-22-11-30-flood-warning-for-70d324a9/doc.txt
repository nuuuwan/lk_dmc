மேலும், இந்நிலைலே வதாடர்பில் மதலவயாை முன்வைச்சரிக்லக நடவடிக்லககலள மேற்வகாள்ளுோறு அைர்த்த முகாலேத்துவப் பிரிவுகளும் மகட்டுக்வகாள்ளப்படுகின்றை.

Due to significant rainfall received in the upper and middle catchment areas of the Kala Oya Basin, the water level of the Kala Oya is currently approaching flood level. Furthermore, the spill gates of the Rajanganaya Reservoir have been opened, and water is currently being released into the Kala Oya at a rate exceeding 13,200 cubic feet per second (cusecs). Depending on the current inflow to the reservoir, the rate of water release may need to be increased further in the coming hours. Under these circumstances, there is a risk of flooding in the lower reaches of the river valley and adjacent low-lying areas within the Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Rajanganaya, Nochchiyagama, Wanathawilluwa and Karuwalagaswewa, located within the basin. The public residing in these areas, as well as drivers and passengers travelling through these areas, are hereby urged to remain highly vigilant and exercise extreme caution in view of this situation.

Furthermore, Disaster Management authorities are requested to take the necessary precautionary measures in response to this situation.
