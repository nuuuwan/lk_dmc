Managing Rain Water --- Make Sri Lanka Prosperous

DI/Hydro/103(V) 2026.11.12

As of today, significant rainfall has been recorded in many areas of the upper and middle catchment of the Nilwala River Basin. Based on the prevailing rainfall conditions and an analysis of water levels recorded at river gauging stations maintained by the Irrigation Department within the Nilwala River Basin, there is a possibility of minor flooding in low-lying areas within the Nilwala River floodplain in the following Divisional Secretariat Divisions during the next 24 hours: Akuressa, Athuraliya, Malimbada, Kamburupitiya, Thihagoda, and Matara. The public residing in these areas, as well as motorists travelling through them, are requested to exercise extreme caution in view of the potential flood risk. Disaster management authorities are requested to take the necessary precautionary measures in response to this situation.
