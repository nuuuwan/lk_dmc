Significant rainfall has been recorded in the upper catchment areas of the Kelani River

Basin. Based on the prevailing rainfall conditions and the analysis of water levels

recorded at the river gauging stations maintained by the Department of Irrigation

within the Kelani River Basin, there is a risk of minor flooding occurring within

the next 24 hours from now in the low-lying areas of the Kelani River Valley falling

within the Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Ruwanwella, Seethawaka, Dompe,

Dehiowita, Eheliyagoda, Yatiyantota and Bulathkohupitiya.

The public residing in these areas and motorists travelling through these areas are

requested to exercise extreme caution in view of this situation.

Furthermore, if heavy rainfall continues, there is a possibility that this flood situation

may also affect the low-lying areas of the Kelani River Valley downstream of

Hanwella.

Disaster Management authorities are requested to take the necessary

precautionary measures in this regard.
