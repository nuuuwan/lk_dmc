Due to the significant rainfall experienced in the upper and middle reaches of the Deduru Oya Basin, the water level of the Deduru Oya is currently approaching flood level. Furthermore, the spillway gates of the Deduru Oya Reservoir have been opened, and water is currently being released into the Deduru Oya at a rate of approximately 16,500 cubic feet per second. Depending on the current inflow to the reservoir, the rate of water release may need to be increased further in the coming period. Under these circumstances, a risk of flooding has been identified in the lower reaches of the river valley and the surrounding low-lying areas within the Wariyapola, Nikaweratiya, Maho, Kobeigane, Bingiriya, Pallama, Chilaw, Arachchikattuwa and Rasnayakapura Divisional Secretariat Divisions located within the Deduru Oya Basin. The public residing in these areas and motorists travelling along roads in the affected areas are strongly advised to remain highly vigilant and exercise extreme caution in view of this situation. The Disaster Management authorities are also requested to take the necessary precautionary measures in response to this situation.
