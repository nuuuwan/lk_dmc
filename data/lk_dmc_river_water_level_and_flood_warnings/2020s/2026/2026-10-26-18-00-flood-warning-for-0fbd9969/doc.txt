மேலுே், இந்நிழலழே பதாடே்பில் ததடவயான முன்கனச்சரிக்டக நடவடிக்டககடை

தமற்ககாை்ளுமாறு அனர்த்த முகாடமத்துவப் பிரிவுகை் தகட்டுக்ககாை்ைப்படுகின்றன. The water level of the Attanagalu Oya is rising due to significant rainfall in the upper and middle catchment areas of the Attanagalu Oya Basin and the commencement of water releases through the spill gates of the Basnagoda Reservoir. Based on this situation and the analysis of water levels recorded at the river gauging stations maintained by the Department of Irrigation within the Attanagalu Oya Basin, there is a risk of flooding in the low-lying areas of the Attanagalu Oya and Uruwal Oya valleys within the Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Divulapitiya, Mirigama, Attanagalla, Mahara, Gampaha, Minuwangoda, Ja-Ela, Katana and Wattala in the coming period. The public residing in these areas and motorists travelling along roads within the affected areas are requested to exercise extreme caution in view of this situation. Furthermore, Disaster Management authorities are requested to take the necessary precautionary measures in this regard.
