Time :- 11.30 A.M මේලාව :- මප.ව 11.30

Significant rainfall has been recorded in the catchment areas of the Kalu Ganga Basin, and rainfall is continuing. Based on the prevailing rainfall conditions and the analysis of water levels recorded at river gauging stations maintained by the Irrigation Department within the Kalu Ganga Basin, a minor flood situation is likely to occur in the low-lying areas of the Kalu Ganga floodplain within the next 24 hours from now. The areas at risk include the following Divisional Secretariat Divisions:

• Ratnapura District: Pelmadulla, Nivithigala, Ratnapura, Kuruwita, Ayagama and Elapatha.

• Kalutara District: Ingiriya, Horana, Dodangoda, Millaniya Bulathsinhala, Madurawala and Palindanuwara The public residing in these areas and drivers travelling through these areas are requested to remain highly vigilant and take appropriate precautions in view of this situation. The Disaster Management authorities are requested to take the necessary precautionary measures in this regard.
