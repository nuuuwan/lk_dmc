WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 17 November 2026

The Cyclone “Akvan” (pronounced: Akvan) over the Southeast Bay of Bengal and adjoining Southwest Bay of Bengal was centred near latitude 12.2°N and longitude 85.7°E (approximately 630 km from Trincomalee) at around 08.30 a.m. on 17th November 2026.

The system is very likely to continue moving north-northwestwards and intensify further into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the Westcentral Bay of Bengal around 05.30 p.m. on 17th November 2026.

Thereafter, it is likely to move northwestwards and further intensify into a Very Severe Cyclonic Storm around 05.30 a.m. on 19th November 2026. Continuing its northwestward movement, the system is likely to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Nellore and Tuni around 11.30 a.m. on 20th November 2026 as a Very Severe Cyclonic Storm.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 06N-18N and 80E-95E, and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places off the coasts extending from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota. Winds:

Winds will be North-westerly or Westerly in the sea areas around the island. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to 60 kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Wind speed may increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the island. State of Sea:

The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly rough at times.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
