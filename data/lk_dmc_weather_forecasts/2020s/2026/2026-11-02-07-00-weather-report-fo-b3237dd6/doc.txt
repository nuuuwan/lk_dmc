එමනිසා උතුරු අක්ාාංශ 10 ත් 17 ත් සහ නැගෙනහිර ගේශාාංශ 80 ත් 90 ත් අතර මුහුදු ප්‍රගේශවල නැවත දැනුම් ගදන තුරු ධීවර සහ නාවුක කටයුතුවලින් වළකින ගලසත් එම මුහුදු ප්‍රගේශවල ධීවර සහ නාවික කටයුතු වල ගයගදන ප්‍රජාවන් ආරක්‍ෂිත ප්‍රගේශ කරා යාත්‍රා කරන ගලසත් උපගදස් ගදනු ලැගේ.

එගස්ම මන්නාරම සිට කන්කසන්තුරය හරහා ත්‍රිකුණාමලය දකවා ෙැඹුරු සහ ගනාෙැඹුරු මුහුදු ප්‍රගේශවලත් නැවත දැනුම් ගදනතුරු ධීවර සහ නාවුක කටයුතුවලින් වැලකී සිටන ගලසත් දැනුම් ගදනු ලැගේ.

Sea Areas Around the island

The Cyclonic Storm “Murasu” over the Westcentral and adjoining Southwest Bay of Bengal was centred near latitude 13.3°N and longitude 83.0°E, about 480 km north-northeast of Kankesanthurai, at 2330 hours on 01 November 2026.

The system is very likely to move west-northwestwards and intensify further into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the Westcentral Bay of Bengal during the next 03 hours. Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards and cross the South Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Narsapur as a Severe Cyclonic Storm between midnight on 02 November and the early hours of 03 November 2026.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 10N-17N and 80E-90E until further notice. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

Furthermore, naval and fishing communities are advised not to venture into the deep and shallow sea areas extending from Mannar to Trincomalee via Kankasanthurai until further notice.

Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards and cross the South Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Narsapur as a Severe Cyclonic Storm between midnight on 02 November and the early hours of 03 November 2026.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 10N-17N and 80E-90E until further notice. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

Furthermore, naval and fishing communities are advised not to venture into the deep and shallow sea areas extending from Mannar to Trincomalee via Kankasanthurai until further notice.
