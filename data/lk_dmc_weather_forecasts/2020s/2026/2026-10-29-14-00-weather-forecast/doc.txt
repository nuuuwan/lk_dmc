Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 29 October 2026

Several spells of showers will occur in Western and Sabaragamuwa provinces and in Galle and Matara districts. A few showers may occur in North-western province.

Showers or thunder showers will occur at a several places in Central, Uva and Eastern provinces and in Polonnaruwa and Hambantota districts after 1.00 p.m. and, fairly heavy falls above 50 mm are likely at some places in these areas. Mainly fair weather conditions are expected in other areas of the Island.

The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in the sea areas around the island.

Winds:

Winds will be South-westerly and wind speed will be (25-35) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to 45 kmph at times in the deep and shallow sea areas extending from Puttalam to Kankasanthurai via Mannar and from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota.

State of Sea: The deep and shallow sea areas extending from Puttalam to Kankasanthurai via Mannar and from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota will be fairly rough at times.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
