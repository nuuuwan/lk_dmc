WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 18 November 2026

The Cyclone “Akvan” over South and adjoining West-central Bay of Bengal was intensified into a severe cyclonic storm and lay centered, near Latitude 12.7°N and Longitude 85.5°E (approximately 650 km northeast to Trincomalee) at around 05.30 a.m. today (18th November 2026).

Continuing to move north-northwestward, it is likely to cross Andhra Pradesh coasts between Nellore and Visakhapatnam, India around 11.30 am of 20th November.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 10N-20N and 80E-95E, until further notice. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Mainly fair weather will prevail in the sea areas around the island.

Winds:

Winds will be North-westerly or Northerly in the sea areas around the island. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to (50-55) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa.

State of Sea:

The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island will be moderate.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
