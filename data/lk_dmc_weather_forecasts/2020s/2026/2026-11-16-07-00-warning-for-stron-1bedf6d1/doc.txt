ශ්‍රී ලංකාවට ඊසාන දද්‍සින්, ගිනිදකාණ දිග ද ංගාල ද ාක්ක මුහුදු ප්‍රදේශය ආශ්‍රිතව පැවති පීඩන අවපාතය ‘ගැඹුරු

පීඩන අවපාතයක්’ ද්‍ක්වා වර්ධනය වී ඇත. ඊදේ ප.ව. 11.30 වන විට දෙෙ පේධතිය, ත්‍රිකුණාෙලය සිට කි.මී. 640 ක්

පෙණ දුරින්, ඊසාන දද්‍සින් උතුරු අක්්ාංශ 10.6° සහ නැදගනහිර දේශාංශ 86.7° ආසන්නදේ ස්ථානගත වී තිබුණි.

තවදුරටත් දෙෙ පේධතිය ශ්‍රී ලංකාදවන් ඉවතට ඇදද්‍මින් අද්‍ දින (දනාවැම් ර් 16) සවස් කාලදේදී ද්‍කුණු සහ යා ද්‍

Bulletin No MSW/26/11/16/01-EN

For Multi-day boats in the West-Central Bay of Bengal deep sea areas and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambantota via Trincomalee and Pottuvil.

The low-pressure area located to the East of Sri Lanka, in the Bay of Bengal, has developed into a ‘Depression.’

As of 5:30 a.m. today, this system was positioned approximately 600 km northeast of Trincomalee. It is highly

likely that the system will continue to move in a north-westwards, staying well off the east coast of Sri Lanka—

and intensify into a ‘Deep Depression’ by tomorrow morning (November 16). By tomorrow night, it is expected

to develop into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ over the southern and adjacent west-central Bay of Bengal, and further

intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded

by 05N-15N and 80E-90E, and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to

Hambanthota via Trincomalee and Pottuvil, until further notice. Further, Naval and fishing

communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued

by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.
