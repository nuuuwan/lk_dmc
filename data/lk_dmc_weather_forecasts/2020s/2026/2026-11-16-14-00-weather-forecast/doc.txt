Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 16 November 2026

The ‘Deep Depression’ over the Bay of Bengal, located to the northeast of Sri Lanka, was centered approximately 645 km from Trincomalee as of 08.30 a.m. today (16 November). It is very likely to continue to move away from Sri Lanka and intensify into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ by the evening of today over the southern and adjoining west-central Bay of Bengal, and further intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17.

Several spells of showers will occur in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western provinces and in the Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya, Galle and Matara districts.

Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in the Uva province and in Ampara district after 2.00 p.m.

Mainly dry weather will prevail over the other areas of the island.

Misty conditions can be expected in Central, Sabaragamuwa and Uva province and in Ampara district of the island during the early morning hours.

Misty conditions may prevail in most parts of the island during the early hours of the morning.

The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 16 November 2026

The ‘Deep Depression’ over the Bay of Bengal, located to the northeast of Sri Lanka, was centered approximately 645 km from Trincomalee as of 08.30 a.m. today (16 November) near Latitude 11.2°N and Longitude 86.4°E. It is very likely to continue to move away from Sri Lanka and intensify into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ by the evening of today over the southern and adjoining west-central Bay of Bengal, and further intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 05N-17N and 80E-95E, and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers may occur at several places in the sea areas around the island.

Winds:

Winds will be North-westerly or Westerly in the sea areas around the island. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to 60 kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Wind speed may increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the island. State of Sea:

The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly rough at times.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
