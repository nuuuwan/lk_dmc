්මාමා පේධති්ේ සෘජු බලංපෑමාක ශ්‍රී ලං කා වට ්න්‍ය මාැතිවුවද එමා පේධතිය ්වත ඇදීයන්‍ය සුළ ප්‍රව හාය දිවයින්‍ය හාරහා පවතින්‍ය බැවින් දිවයි්න් නිරිතදිග ප්‍ර්ේශ්ේ වැසි තත්ත්ව්ේ වැඩි වීමාක ඉදිරිදින්‍ය කිහිප්ේ දී අ්ේකෂ ්කා්ර්.

WEATHER FORECAST FOR 25 October 2026

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 24 October 2026

The low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal in the vicinity of the North Andaman Islands is very likely to intensify into a depression by today (24). The system is expected to move west-northwestwards and intensify further into a deep depression by 25 October and into a cyclonic storm over the west-central Bay of Bengal by the morning of 26 October 2026.

Although this system is not expected to have a direct impact on Sri Lanka, an increase in rainfall is expected over the southwestern parts of the island during the next few days, as the wind flow associated with the system extends across the island.

Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in Western, Sabaragamuwa, Northern and North-western provinces and in Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts and, heavy falls about 100 mm are likely at some places in these areas.

Showers or thunder showers will occur at several places after 1.00 p.m. in the Uva province and in Ampara and Batticaloa districts.

Fairly strong winds of about (30-40) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the Central hills, Northern, North-central and North-western provinces and in Trincomalee, Hambantota and Matale Districts.

The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 24 October 2026

The low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal in the vicinity of the North Andaman Islands is very likely to intensify into a depression by today (24). The system is expected to move west-northwestwards and intensify further into a deep depression by 25 October and into a cyclonic storm over the west-central Bay of Bengal by the morning of 26 October 2026.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 10N-20N and 85E-95E until further notice. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas. The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in the sea areas around the island.

Winds:

Winds will be South-westerly and Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to (50-60) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Trincomalee to Pottuvil via Kankasanthurai, Puttalam, Colombo, Galle and Hambantota.

State of Sea: The sea areas off the coasts extending from Trincomalee to Pottuvil via Kankasanthurai, Puttalam, Colombo, Galle and Hambantota will be rough at times. Other sea areas around the island may be moderate.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
