එමනිසා උතුරු අක්ාංශ 10 ත් 20 ත් සහ නැමගනහිර මේශාංශ 80 ත් 95 ත් අතර මුහුදු ප්‍රමේශවල නැවත දැනුේ මදන තුරු ධීවර සහ නාවුක කටයුතුවලින්ත වළකින මලසත් එම මුහුදු ප්‍රමේශවල ධීවර සහ නාවික කටයුතු වල මයමදන ප්‍රජාවන්ත ආරක්‍ෂිත ප්‍රමේශ කරා යාත්‍රා කරන මලසත් උපමදස් මදනු ලැමබ්.

Bulletin No: 07 MSW/26/10/27/01 COLOR: Red Warning for strong winds and rough seas.

Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre Issued at 08.00 a.m. 27 October 2026, valid for the next 12 hours.

For the Bay of Bengal and deep and shallow sea areas around the island.

Take Action! For Multiday Boats The Severe Cyclonic Storm “Arnab” over West central Bay of Bengal intensified into a ‘Very Severe Cyclonic Storm’ and lay centered at 02.30 a.m. of today, the 27th October, 2026 over the near latitude 15.9°N and longitude 85.4°E about 890 km to the north-east of Kankasanthurai. It is very likely to continue to move west-northwestwards till 05.30 p.m. of 27th October and thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards and cross the Indian land through Andhra Pradesh coast as a very severe cyclonic storm by 11.30 a.m. of 28th October, 2026.

Although this system is not expected to have a direct impact on Sri Lanka, the showery condition over the southwestern parts of the island is expected continue today, as the wind flow associated with the system extends across the island. Wind speeds in the sea areas marked under the “Warning” category may increase to 80-140 kmph at times, heavy thundershowers can occur, the wave height may increase about (3.0 – 4.0) meters in the mentioned sea areas and the sea conditions in these areas will become very rough.
