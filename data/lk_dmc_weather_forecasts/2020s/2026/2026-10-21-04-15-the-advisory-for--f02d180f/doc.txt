Bulletin No: 05 MLA/26/10/21/01 COLOR: Amber

Advisory for Rough Seas Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre Issued at 04.15 a.m. on 21 October 2026 Valid for the period until 10.15 a.m. 21 October 2026.

For the sea areas extending from to Kankasanthurai to Hambantota

via Kalpitiya, Colombo and Galle. PLEASE BE PREPARED! Thundershowers will occur in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambantota via Kalpitiya, Colombo and Galle.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.

The naval and fishing communities who are engaged in fishing and naval activities in the above sea areas are requested to be vigilant in this regard.
