PLEASE BE PREPARED!

A low level atmospheric disturbance

near Sri Lanka has developed into a

low-pressure area. Under the influence

of this system, an increase in rainfall is

expected over the island during the

next few days from today (22).

Heavy falls above 100 mm are likely at

some places in Western,

Sabaragamuwa, Central, North-

western, Northern and Southern

provinces and in Anuradhapura district.
