Advisory for Rough Seas Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre Issued at 05.00 p.m. 12 October 2026 Valid for the period until 01.00 a.m. 13 October 2026.

For the sea areas extending from Mannar to Matara via Colombo. PLEASE BE PREPARED!

Thundershowers will occur in the sea areas off the coast extending from Mannar to Matara via Colombo.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.

The naval and fishing communities who are engaged in fishing and naval activities in the above sea areas are requested to be vigilant in this regard.
