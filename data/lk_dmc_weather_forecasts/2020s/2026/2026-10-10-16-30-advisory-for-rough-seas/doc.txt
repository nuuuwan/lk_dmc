For the sea areas extending from Mannar to Hambanthota via

Colombo and Galle. PLEASE BE PREPARED! Showers or thundershowers will occur in the sea areas off the coast extending from Mannar to Hambanthota via Colombo and Galle during the evening or night. Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
