WEATHER FORECAST FOR 16 NOVEMBER 2026

Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 16 November 2026.

The Depression located to the Northeast of Sri Lanka, in the Bay of Bengal, has developed into a ‘Deep Depression’. The system was located approximately 640 km northeast of Trincomalee, by 11:30 p.m. yesterday. It is highly likely that the system will continue to move away from Sri Lanka and intensify into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ by the evening of today (November 16) over the southern and adjoining west-central Bay of Bengal, and further intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17. Prevailing showers over the island is expected to reduce from today (16). Several spells of showers will occur in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, North-western and Southern provinces and in the Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts.

Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in the Uva province and in Ampara district after 2.00 p.m.

Mainly dry weather will prevail over the other areas of the island. The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Misty conditions may prevail in most parts of the island during the early morning hours.

WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 16 November 2026.

The Depression located to the Northeast of Sri Lanka, in the Bay of Bengal, has developed into a ‘Deep Depression’. The system was located approximately 640 km northeast of Trincomalee, by 11:30 p.m. yesterday. It is highly likely that the system will continue to move away from Sri Lanka and intensify into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ by the evening of today (November 16) over the southern and adjacent west-central Bay of Bengal, and further intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 05N-15N and 80E-90E, and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambanthota via Trincomalee and Pottuvil. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers will occur in the sea areas off the coast extending from Colombo to Hambantota via Galle and Matara.

Winds:

Winds will be North-westerly or Westerly in the sea areas around the island. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to 60 kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambantota via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Wind speed may increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the island. State of Sea:

The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambantota via Trincomalee and Batticaloa will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly rough at times.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
