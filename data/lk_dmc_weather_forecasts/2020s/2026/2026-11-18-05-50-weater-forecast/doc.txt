WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 18 November 2026.

The Cyclone “Akvan” (pronounced: Akvan) over the Southeast Bay of Bengal and adjoining Southwest Bay of Bengal was centred near latitude 12.6°N and longitude 85.6°E (approximately 650 km from Trincomalee) at around 02.30 a.m. on 18th November 2026. The Cyclone is very likely to continue move slowly north-northwestwards and intensify further into a “Severe Cyclonic Storm” over the Westcentral Bay of Bengal around 05.30 a.m. on 18th November 2026. Thereafter, it is likely to move northwestwards and intensify further into a “very severe cyclonic storm” around 05.30 p.m. of 19th November, 2026. Continuing to move northwestward, it is likely to cross Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts between Nellore and Visakhapatnam on 20th November, 2026 as a Very severe cyclonic storm. Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 06N-19N and 80E-95E, and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa, until further notice. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas. The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in the sea areas off the coasts extending from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota during the evening or night. Mainly fair weather will prevail in the other sea areas around the island. Winds:

Winds will be North-westerly or Westerly in the sea areas around the island. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to 60 kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Wind speed may increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the island. State of Sea:

The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Pottuvil via Trincomalee and Batticaloa will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly rough at times.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
