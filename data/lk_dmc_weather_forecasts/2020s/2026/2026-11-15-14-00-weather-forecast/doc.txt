WEATHER FORECAST FOR 16 November 2026

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 15 November 2026

The low-pressure area located to the East of Sri Lanka, in the Bay of Bengal, has developed into a ‘Depression. The system was located approximately 630 km northeast of Trincomalee, slightly East of the Northeast direction by 11:30 a.m. today. It is highly likely that the system will continue to move away from Sri Lanka and intensify into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ by the evening of tomorrow (November 16) over the southern and adjacent west-central Bay of Bengal, and further intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17. Due to the influence of this system, cloudy skies will prevail over the entire island tomorrow. Several spells of showers will occur in the Northern, North-central, Eastern, Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western provinces and in the Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya, Galle and Matara districts.

Showers or thundershowers may occur a few places in the Uva province and Hambantota district after 2.00 p.m.

The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Misty conditions may prevail in most parts of the island during the early morning hours.

WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS

Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 15 November 2026

The low-pressure area located to the East of Sri Lanka, in the Bay of Bengal, has developed into a ‘Depression.’, The system was located approximately 630 km Northeast of Trincomalee, slightly East of the Northeast direction, by 11:30 a.m. today. It is highly likely that the system will continue to move in a North-westwards, staying well off the east coast of Sri Lanka—and intensify into a ‘Deep Depression’ by tomorrow morning (November 16). By tomorrow night, it is expected to develop into a ‘Cyclonic Storm’ over the southern and adjacent west-central Bay of Bengal, and further intensify into a ‘Severe Cyclonic Storm’ over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 05N-15N and 80E-90E, and the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambanthota via Trincomalee and Pottuvil. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.

The naval and fishing communities, are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.

Condition of Rain:

Showers or thundershowers will occur in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Galle via Trincomalee, Batticaloa and Hambantota.

Winds:

Winds will be North-westerly or Northerly in the sea areas around the island. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.

Wind speed can increase up to 60 kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambantota via Trincomalee and Batticaloa. Wind speed may increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the island. State of Sea:

The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Hambantota via Trincomalee and Batticaloa will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly rough at times.

Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70 kmph) and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
