Special Weather Bulletin. Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre, Department of Meteorology. Issued at 05.00 p.m. on 22 October 2026

PLEASE BE AWARE!

A low-pressure area is likely to form over Southeast and adjoining East-central Bay of Bengal around 23rd October, 2026. Then it is likely to move west-northwestwards and intensify further.

Therefore, Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the deep-sea areas bounded by 10N-20N and 80E-95E. Further, Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are advised to move to safe areas.
