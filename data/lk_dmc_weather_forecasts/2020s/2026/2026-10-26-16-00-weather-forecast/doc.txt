GENERAL WEATHER FORECAST FOR NEXT 36 HOURS

Issued at 04.00 p.m. on 26 October 2026

The Deep Depression over East-central and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal has intensified into cyclonic storm “Arnab”. It is moved westwards and lay centered at 8.30 a.m. of today, the 26th October, 2026, near Latitude 15.4°N and Longitude 88.5°E about 1100 km. to the north-east of Kankasanthurai. It is very likely to move nearly west-northwestwards, and intensify further into a severe cyclonic storm over west central Bay of Bengal by 05.30 pm of 26th October. This system will intensify further into a very severe cyclonic storm on 27th October, and this will landfall on the Indian coast through Andhra Pradesh around 11:30 a.m. on the 28th.

Although this system is not expected to have a direct impact on Sri Lanka, the showery condition is expected to continue over the southwestern parts of the island during the next few days, as the wind flow associated with the system extends across the island.

Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in Northern, Western, Sabaragamuwa, Southern, North-western, Central and North-central provinces and in Trincomalee districts and, fairly heavy falls above 75 mm are likely at some places in these areas.

Showers or thunder showers will occur at elsewhere after 1.00 p.m.

Strong winds of about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the Central hills, Northern, North-central and North-western provinces and in Trincomalee and Hambantota Districts.

The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
