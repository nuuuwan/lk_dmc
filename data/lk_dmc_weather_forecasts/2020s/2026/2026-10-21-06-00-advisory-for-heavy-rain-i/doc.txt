Bulletin No: 02 LRA/26/10/21/01 COLOR: Amber

Advisory for Heavy Rain Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre Issued at 5:30 a.m. on 21 October 2026, valid for the period until 5.30 a.m. on 22 October 2026

For Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, North-western, Northern and

Southern provinces and in Anuradhapura district

PLEASE BE PREPARED!

A low level atmospheric disturbance

near Sri Lanka is likely to develop into a

low-pressure area within the next 24

hours. Under the influence of this

system, an increase in rainfall is

expected over the island during the

next few days from today (21).

Heavy falls above 100 mm are likely at

some places in Western,

Sabaragamuwa, Central, North-

western, Northern and Southern

provinces and in Anuradhapura district.
