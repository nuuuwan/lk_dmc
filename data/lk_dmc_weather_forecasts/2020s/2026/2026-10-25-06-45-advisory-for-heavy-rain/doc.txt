Advisory for Heavy Rain Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre Issued at 5:30 a.m. on 25 October 2026, valid for the period until 5.30 a.m. on 26 October 2026

For Western, Sabaragamuwa, North-western, Northern and Southern

provinces and in Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya and Anuradhapura districts

PLEASE BE PREPARED!

The low-pressure area over the Bay of

Bengal in the vicinity of the North

Andaman Islands has intensified into a

depression. Under the influence of this

system, an increase in rainfall is

expected over the island during the

next few days from today (25).

Heavy falls above 100 mm are likely at

some places in Western,

Sabaragamuwa, North-western,

Northern and Southern provinces and

in Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya and

Anuradhapura districts.
